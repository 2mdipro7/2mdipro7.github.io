# Portfolio visual assets

Final artwork and web exports are saved in this folder. These covers are explanatory illustrations, not operational screenshots or measured model outputs. Generated masters are preserved under `masters/`; `asset-dimensions.json` records actual dimensions. SVG sources are scalable. Built-in imagegen outputs for Aves, Story Genre and LeadHarvest have smaller native dimensions than Higgsfield's 4K-tier outputs; no native 4K claim is made for those files.

## Providers

Higgsfield: UAE, Mammal World, Credit Union, Reporting Automation and the widescreen video poster. Built-in imagegen: Aves, Story Genre, LeadHarvest. Code-native SVG: library, SEO, education, pricing and ecommerce covers and supporting diagrams.
